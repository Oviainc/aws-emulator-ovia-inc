Cause 6 of 10: the ObjectIds returned by CreateCommit, PutFile and GetDifferences are not the git object ids of the content they describe (residual checks).

Cause 8 of 10: GetCommit with an explicitly empty commitId is classified as a missing field instead of a malformed identifier.
